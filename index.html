How to play

  1. The Sphinx asks 3 riddles each day: easy, medium, and hard.
  2. Type your answer. You get one try per riddle.
  3. Get 2 right and you walk free. Get fewer and you are eaten.
  4. Earn points for correct answers, surviving, and daily streaks.
  5. Spend points in the Shop on gods. Each gives a power, but has a drawback.
  6. New riddles arrive every day at midnight.
